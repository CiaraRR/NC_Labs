Question 1 — Coffee Shop Orders

  • Ask the user how many coffees they want to order.
  • Use a for loop to process each item.
  • Ask for 1 for Small (€2.50) or 2 for Large (€4.00).
  • Display the selected item and its cost.
  • Display “Invalid choice” for another number.
  • Calculate and display the total on a receipt.
  • Use Scanner for input and DecimalFormat for prices in euros.
